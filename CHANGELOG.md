# Changelog

## 0.0.10 (unreleased)

First version for testing, Fabric 26.3.

- Hot Air Balloon: crafted from wool, string, a campfire and wooden slabs, set up on the ground, inflates in two seconds.
- Flight: burner to climb, vent to sink, hover in between, slow turning and thrust; wind per height band that turns over time, gentle low down and nearly twice your own thrust up high, and while you pilot it leans toward where the balloon is heading, so most heights help you on your way; gusts sweep through single layers now and then (5-11 s, two to three times the wind, with a whirl and rushing air), the wind lines grow with them; wind lines stream past the basket the way the wind carries you, and a wind arrow beside the hotbar shows where the wind takes you (green = where you look), your ground speed, and a chevron when the air above or below would carry you faster; no fall damage for riders; the pilot stands at the burner (also with Fresh Animations Player); slow falling if the balloon is destroyed in the air.
- Four seats; mobs that walk into a resting basket climb in; leashed mobs can be tied to the balloon and are lifted on the rope.
- A chest strapped to the basket, opened with the inventory key while riding or by sneak-using the balloon.
- Dyeable envelope (base and stripes) and banner emblems on the front and back; the look stays on the item.
- Mooring: tie a balloon to any fence with a lead (the rope is tied to the rim of the basket) and, with nobody at the controls, it floats up on its own to hover at the end of the rope, swaying with the wind, without fuel.
- Burner fuel from any furnace fuel, refilled from the pilot's inventory or the chest; heat and fuel gauges beside the hotbar.
- Advancement "Up, Up and Away"; settings in Mod Menu and `config/hotairballoons.json`.
