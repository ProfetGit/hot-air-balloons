# Changelog

## 0.0.10 (unreleased)

First version for testing, Fabric 26.3.

- Hot Air Balloon: crafted from wool, string, a campfire and wooden slabs, set up on the ground, inflates in two seconds.
- Flight: burner to climb, vent to sink, hover in between, slow turning and thrust; wind per height band that turns over time; no fall damage for riders; slow falling if the balloon is destroyed in the air.
- Four seats; mobs that walk into a resting basket climb in; leashed mobs can be tied to the balloon and are lifted on the rope.
- A chest strapped to the basket, opened with the inventory key while riding or by sneak-using the balloon.
- Dyeable envelope (base and stripes) and banner emblems on the front and back; the look stays on the item.
- Mooring Post: tie a balloon with a lead and it holds its place, even aloft, without fuel.
- Burner fuel from any furnace fuel, refilled from the pilot's inventory or the chest; heat and fuel gauges beside the hotbar.
- Advancement "Up, Up and Away"; settings in Mod Menu and `config/hotairballoons.json`.
