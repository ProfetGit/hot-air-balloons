# Changelog

## 0.0.25

First public beta, Fabric 26.3. Needed on the server and on every player's game, with Fabric API.

- Hot Air Balloon: craft it from wool, string, a campfire and wooden slabs, set it up on the ground, and it inflates in two seconds.
- Flight: Space is the burner (climb), Ctrl is the vent (sink), it hovers in between; turn with left/right, a slow push with forward/back. The burner stops climbing before your head reaches a roof, and tells you.
- Wind that blows a different way at every height and turns over the days, with gusts. Wind lines stream past the basket and a wind arrow beside the hotbar shows which way a better layer is.
- Walk around the basket while it flies; four seats (three with a chest). Animals that walk in while it stands on the ground ride in the corners.
- A chest on board, opened with the inventory key while you fly.
- Dye the envelope and its stripes, or put a banner on it: the envelope takes the banner's colours and shows the emblem on all four sides. A dyed balloon keeps its look when you pick it up.
- Mooring: tie a balloon to any fence with a lead and it floats up on its own and hovers at the end of the rope.
- Burner fuel: coal, charcoal, wood, blaze rods, anything a furnace burns, refilled from your inventory or the chest. Creative players need none.
- Short hints for the controls, an empty tank and a spot with no room.
- Nobody aboard takes fall damage. Advancement "Up, Up and Away". Settings in Mod Menu and `config/hotairballoons.json`.
