# mp-guest: the second player walks up to the pilot's balloon, steps in, and rides along in the basket.
world wait
wait 20
log guest in world
until chat~BOARDNOW timeout=900
wait 10
until target~hot_air_balloon timeout=200
state guest_before
shot guest_sees_balloon
use
wait 15
state guest_aboard
log guest aboard
shot guest_in_basket
until y>-55 timeout=900
state guest_climbing
shot guest_climb
wait 80
state guest_high
shot guest_high
camera back
wait 6
shot guest_back
camera first
until y<-59.5 timeout=1500
wait 20
state guest_landed
shot guest_landed
hold forward,jump 25
wait 20
state guest_out
end
