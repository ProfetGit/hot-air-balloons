Felt right:
- Recipe is findable: a campfire unlocks it ("New Recipe(s) Unlocked!" toast with the balloon icon).
- Place then click boards in one motion.
- Open-field flight: climb ~2.4 b/s, W thrust moves it, A turns it, Sprint vents ~2 b/s; gauges show heat, hover tick, fuel, speed.
- Riders never take the balloon's fall: vent landing from ~24 blocks and an out-of-fuel sink from ~30 end at health 20, zero hurt events.
- Shift in the air keeps you standing in the basket (no damage, no text). Walking over the rim 19 blocks up cost 16 hp: the player's own choice.
- Water: floats at the surface (y -60.12), climbs and lands back on the water.
- MP (dedicated server, allow-flight false): the second player boards the pilot's balloon, rides 24 blocks up and down, no hurt, no kick, 0 "moved wrongly" (`mt.py HotAirBalloons mp --case playtest`: 27/27).
- `--addon fa` (FA + FA Player): the pilot stands upright with hands on the rim while seated, climbing, thrusting, turning, and after Shift. No mid-air sitting.
