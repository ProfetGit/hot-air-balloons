world wait
wait 20
log pilot in world
until hand=hotairballoons:hot_air_balloon timeout=600
state pilot_has_item
slot 1
look 0 60
wait 20
state pilot_aim
use
wait 50
state pilot_placed
shot pilot_placed
use
wait 15
state pilot_seated
log pilot seated
until chat~CLIMBNOW timeout=900
camera back
wait 10
state pilot_with_guest
shot pilot_sees_guest
clip pilot_climb 120
hold jump 240
state pilot_climb
shot pilot_aloft
wait 40
hold sprint 300
state pilot_vent
hold sprint 160
state pilot_landing
wait 60

log pilot landed
hold sneak 6
wait 80
end
